How the levels work

Each corpus (the Esperanto Wikipedia and the Tekstaro) has a ranked list of its 15,000 most frequent lemmas. A word's level comes from the less frequent of its two ranks, and a word in only one list is level 7. Requiring a word to be common in both keeps everyday words together.

Frequency, not CEFR. Levels say what to learn first, not how hard a word is. Half of the lemmas (mostly rarer words and names) have no gloss yet, because Wiktionary has no entry for them; none is invented.

Data: lexicon.json, lexicon.csv, affixes.json. Sources and credit are in the attribution file.
